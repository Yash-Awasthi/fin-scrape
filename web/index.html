THE THESIS

Turn global events into auditable market calls.

01

Ingest

Geopolitics + world news across wire, gov, and intel tiers, plus keyless data APIs — quakes, conflict, disasters, crypto.

02

Resolve

Which companies, tickers, and sectors the event moves — role-tagged primary / competitor / supplier / regulator.

03

Judge

First- and second-order impact, a verdict (INVEST / PULL_OUT / OBSERVE / CAUTIOUS) and a −5…+5 score.

04

Prove

Every call scored against the realized price move — hit-rate, by-verdict, and an equity curve you can audit.
